Reba.

The six weeks after birth are when mothers die and nobody is watching. This puts a trained eye in the room — the family's.

Kinyarwanda: look, watch  ·  A postpartum danger-sign companion for the 42 days after discharge.

60%+of maternal deaths happen after birth

45%of those, within the first 24 hours

9%of mothers are seen within 2 days (LMIC)

28%of maternal deaths: haemorrhage (sub-Saharan Africa)

The problem

She is discharged 24 hours after giving birth and goes home. From that moment until her postnatal check — if she gets one — nobody is monitoring her.

The family is standing right there. They just cannot tell normal from emergency, because every danger sign is also something a new mother is expected to have: bleeding, headache, swelling, fever, feeling low. So they wait.

Haemorrhage kills in 2–3 hours. Pre-eclampsia can appear up to six weeks after delivery. It is a recognition problem, held by untrained people, on a clock measured in hours.

Target market

  • Users: the husband, mother or sister of each of Rwanda's ~400,000 mothers a year — for 42 days.
  • Buyer: Ministry of Health, RSSB, and CHW-programme funders. Avoided emergency admissions are their line item, not the family's.
  • Beachhead, not ceiling: sub-Saharan Africa carries 70% of global maternal deaths; the 2-day coverage gap is region-wide. Rwanda is where it can be proven, because its CHW network is the strongest.

The solution

A 30-second daily check, answered by tapping, typing, or speaking — in Kinyarwanda or English, detected automatically. It returns one of three things, and only three:

▲ Go nowNearest facility, immediately

● Go todaySeen by a health worker today

— WatchRe-check in N hours

Urgent verdicts generate a structured handover summary for the facility, and her community health worker's board turns red live.

How it works

Speak, type or tapKinyarwanda or English, detected automatically

Gemini reads the signsconstrained to the protocol's fixed sign list

The family confirmsnothing is scored until they read it back

Engine decides & shows whyprotocol floor → combinations → escalation only

Core features

  • Voice intake in Kinyarwanda — browsers have no Kinyarwanda ASR, so audio goes to Gemini, which transcribes and reads the signs in one pass.
  • Confirm before scoring — the family reads back what Reba heard before anything is triaged.
  • Escalation-only personalization — her ANC risk factors can raise urgency, never lower it.
  • Every verdict shows its derivation — protocol floor, then each escalation with its reason.
  • Live CHW watch board — cards re-sort by urgency over SSE, no refresh.
  • Live evaluation page — golden vignettes scored on every load.

Responsible AI & ethics

  1. The model never decides. Gemini only maps words onto protocol sign IDs, enum-constrained so an off-protocol sign is unrepresentable. The verdict is pure TypeScript from the WHO postnatal danger-sign protocol.
  2. Personalization can only escalate. Enforced by a monotonic max — there is no de-escalation function in the codebase, proven by a test brute-forcing all 19 signs against all 9 risk factors.
  3. Uncertainty escalates. "Not sure" is scored exactly as "yes". There is no "you are fine" verdict — the floor is Watch, and Reba never tells anyone not to seek care.
